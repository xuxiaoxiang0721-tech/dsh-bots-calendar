# Privacy policy

DSH Bots Calendar is a private tool. Its only user is its owner, Eric.

What it reads and writes: the owner's own Google Calendar events, after he grants access himself.

Where the data goes: between Google and the owner's own computer, where his assistant bots run. To answer him, those bots may pass calendar details to the AI model services he has chosen for them (such as DeepSeek or Anthropic). Nothing is sold, used for advertising, or given to anyone else.

What is stored: the access grant stays on the owner's computer. He can revoke it at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

Questions: open an issue in this repository.

## 中文

这个工具只有 Eric 本人用。他本人授权后，它读取、新建他自己的 Google 日历日程。数据在 Google 和他自己的电脑之间传；他的 Bot 回答他时，相关日程可能经过他选用的模型服务（如 DeepSeek、Anthropic）。不出售，不用于广告，也不给其他人。授权凭证只存在他的电脑上，他随时可以在 Google 账号设置里撤销。
